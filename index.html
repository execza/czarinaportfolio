02 / SERVICES

Expertise & Skills

I use Meta Business Suite to plan, schedule, and publish posts so your content goes out consistently and on time. I can also pick up other scheduling tools quickly, depending on what you prefer.

I handle basic editing on Canva to create clean, eye-catching social posts, graphics, and other marketing materials. I can customize templates or design from scratch, depending on your style and preferences.

I mainly work in Google Workspace and can adapt to other tools depending on your needs. I use Google Sheets for content calendars and trackers, and Google Docs for guides and other materials, to keep your workflow organized.
